# VolunteerHub - Feature Showcase Video Script

**Total Duration: 8-10 minutes**  
**Target Audience: Organizations and High School Volunteers**

---

## Opening Scene (0:00 - 0:30)

**[Screen: VolunteerHub homepage with clean, modern design]**

**Narrator (Voice Over):**
"This is VolunteerHub - a platform that brings together high school students and organizations that need volunteers. Students can find opportunities to complete their community service hours, while organizations connect with enthusiastic young volunteers ready to make a difference. Let's see how it works for both sides."

**[Transition: Split screen showing organization dashboard and volunteer job listings]**

---

## Part 1: For Organizations (0:30 - 4:30)

### Account Setup & Dashboard (0:30 - 1:00)

**[Screen: Organization registration form]**

**Narrator:**
"Getting started as an organization is relatively intuitive. You just need to fill out your basic details - organization name, what you do, and how people can reach you." !

**[Screen: Completed organization dashboard showing overview]**

"Once you're in, you'll land on your dashboard. This is your home base where you can post new opportunities, see who's applied, and keep track of all your volunteers." *

### Creating Job Postings (1:00 - 2:15)

**[Screen: "Post New Job" button clicked, form appears]**

**Narrator:**
"Posting a volunteer opportunity is simple. Start with the basics - give it a clear title like 'Youth Camp Leader' that tells volunteers exactly what they'll be doing."

**[Screen: Filling out job details step by step]**

"Then you'll add the important details:
- Pick a category - this one would be Youth Programs
- Say whether it's in-person or virtual
- Choose your dates and times using the calendar
- Let people know how many helpers you need"

**[Screen: Rich text editor for job description]**

"Here's where you really sell the opportunity. Explain what volunteers will actually be doing - maybe leading activities, mentoring kids, or helping with arts and crafts. Make it sound fun and meaningful."

**[Screen: Requirements section with chips]**

"If you have specific needs, add them as requirements - things like 'Must be comfortable with children' or 'Previous camp experience preferred.' You can add as many as you need."

**[Screen: Job status options]**

"You can save it as a draft if you're not ready to publish, or go live immediately when you want to start getting applications."

### Managing Applications (2:15 - 3:30)

**[Screen: Job posting with "5 Applications" notification]**

**Narrator:**
This is the dashboard after applications come in. Click on the Youth Camp Leader posting to see everyone who's interested."

**[Screen: Application details view]**

**Narrator:**
"Each application gives you the full picture:
- Why they want to volunteer (their cover letter)
- When they're available (shown on a calendar)
- What skills they bring to the table
- Their resume if they uploaded one
- References if they provided any"

**[Screen: Approval process with date range selection]**

"Here's the cool part - when you approve someone, you can pick exactly which dates they'll work. So if your camp runs for two weeks but they're only available for one, no problem. Just select those specific dates."

**[Screen: Welcome message composition]**

"You can also send them a welcome message with all the details they need - where to meet, what to bring, who to ask for on their first day."

**[Screen: Email notification preview]**

"They'll get an email with everything automatically, so you don't have to remember to follow up."

### Job Management Features (3:30 - 4:30)

**[Screen: Edit job interface]**

**Narrator:**
"If you need to change something, you can just edit your posting anytime - maybe the camp dates shifted, or you need more volunteers than you thought."

**[Screen: Job status management]**

"You can also control whether you're still accepting applications. Close it when you have enough people, mark it complete when the camp's over, or delete it entirely if plans change."

**[Screen: Application overview with status indicators]**

"Everything stays organized with status indicators, so you can see at a glance which applications are still pending, who you've approved, and who you had to turn down."

---

## Part 2: For Volunteers (4:30 - 8:00)

### Discovering Opportunities (4:30 - 5:15)

**[Screen: Volunteer job listings page with multiple opportunities]**

**Narrator:**
"Now let's flip to the student side. When you log in as a volunteer, you'll see all the opportunities available right now. Everything's laid out clearly - who's posting it, what the job is, where it is, and when it's happening."

**[Screen: Filter and search functionality]**

"You can narrow things down pretty easily:
- Filter by what you're interested in - maybe you love working with kids, so you'd pick Youth Programs
- Search by where you can get to
- Look for stuff that fits your schedule
- Or just search for something specific"

**[Screen: Individual job posting detail view showing Youth Camp Leader]**

"Click on something like this Youth Camp Leader position and you'll get the full story - exactly what you'll be doing, what skills they're looking for, and what kind of impact you'll make."

### Creating Applications (5:15 - 7:00)

**[Screen: Application form opening for Youth Camp Leader position]**

**Narrator:**
"Applying is pretty straightforward, but there's room to really show who you are. Start with your cover letter - this is where you explain why you want to help with their youth camp and what experience you have with kids."

**[Screen: Interactive calendar for date selection]**

**Narrator:**
"Then pick your available dates using the calendar. Maybe the camp runs for two weeks but you can only do one - just select those dates. The calendar won't let you pick dates that have already passed."

**[Screen: Availability notes field next to calendar]**

"There's also a notes section where you can add details about your schedule - like if you can only do mornings, or if you need to leave early on certain days."

**[Screen: Skills entry system]**

"Add any relevant skills by typing and hitting Enter. Things like 'Great with kids,' 'CPR certified,' or 'Arts and crafts experience' - whatever makes you a good fit for being a camp leader."

**[Screen: References section]**

"If you have a teacher, coach, or anyone who can vouch for you, add their info in the references section. It's optional but it helps."

**[Screen: File upload interface]**

"You can also upload a resume if you have one. We take PDFs, Word docs, or even just a photo of a printed resume."

**[Screen: Application submission confirmation]**

"Double-check everything and hit submit. You'll get a confirmation right away and can track your application status."

### Application Tracking & Communication (7:00 - 7:45)

**[Screen: Application status dashboard]**

**Narrator:**
"After you apply, you can keep track of everything in one place:
- Pending means they're still looking at applications
- Approved means you got the gig - you'll see exactly which dates you're scheduled for
- If they can't use you this time, they might leave feedback to help with future applications"

**[Screen: Approval notification email for Youth Camp Leader position]**

"When you get approved for something like the Youth Camp Leader position, you'll get an email with all the details - when to show up, where to go, what to bring, and who to ask for."

**[Screen: Organization contact information]**

"Plus you'll have their contact info if you need to ask questions or something comes up."

## Best Practices & Tips (7:45 - 8:30)

**[Screen: Split view showing both organization and volunteer perspectives]**

**Narrator:**
"A couple quick tips to get the most out of VolunteerHub:

Organizations - be specific about what volunteers will actually be doing. Instead of just saying 'help with camp,' maybe say 'lead games, assist with crafts, and help with snack time.' The more detail you give, the better applications you'll get.

Students - take a few minutes to write a real cover letter. Don't just say 'I want volunteer hours.' Explain why you're interested in working with kids or why this particular organization caught your eye."

**[Screen: Success example showing Youth Camp Leader application]**

"When both sides put in a little effort, you get great matches - organizations find enthusiastic volunteers who really want to be there, and students find opportunities they're genuinely excited about."

**Narrator:**

**[Screen: Website URL and key features summary]**

## Production Notes

### Visual Elements to Include:
- **Screen recordings** of actual platform navigation
- **Close-up shots** of key features (calendar selection, file upload, application details)
- **Smooth transitions** between organization and volunteer workflows
- **Clean, professional UI** highlighting the modern design

### Technical Specifications:
- **Resolution**: 1080p minimum (4K preferred for platform clarity)
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps for smooth screen recordings
- **Audio**: Clear voiceover with background music at low volume
- **Captions**: Include closed captions for accessibility

### Key Features to Highlight Visually:
1. **Interactive calendar** with date range selection
2. **File upload system** with drag-and-drop functionality
3. **Skills tagging system** with dynamic entry
4. **Application management** with sidebar navigation
5. **Email notification system** with preview
6. **Status indicators** throughout the platform
7. **Mobile responsiveness** (show brief mobile views)

### Suggested B-Roll:
- Students volunteering at various organizations
- Organizations working with volunteers
- Community impact shots
- Quick montages of platform usage on different devices

### Call-to-Action Options:
- **Website visit**: "Visit [your-domain].com to get started"
- **Demo request**: "Schedule a demo to see VolunteerHub in action"
- **Sign-up incentive**: "Join today and connect with your community"

This script showcases all major features while maintaining engagement and demonstrating real value for both user types. The timing allows for detailed feature demonstration while keeping the video concise and professional.
